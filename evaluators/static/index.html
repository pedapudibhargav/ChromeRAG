Why this matters. AI assistants answer questions from web pages by reading a text copy of each page, not the page itself. They cut that text into short pieces, pick the few pieces that best match a question, and answer only from those. So if a fact is missing from the text, the assistant cannot answer; and if menus, ads or text about other things are left in, they crowd out the real content or get repeated as if they were facts.

Content is what the page exists to give: its main text, facts, prices, steps, tables, and on a forum the question and answers. Noise is everything else: menus, footers, cookie and sign-up notices, share buttons, ads, and links or teasers for other pages.

Noise is acceptable if it would not change an answer: levels 3 to 5. Press Esc or Guide to close.
