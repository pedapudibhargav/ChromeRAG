ABOUT THIS STUDY
================

Please read this before you start, and reply by email as described at the end.

Thanks for helping. This rating task is part of a study for a journal paper about tools that
pull the main text out of web pages.

What you will do
  Compare text outputs against web pages in an app on your own computer: {N_ITEMS} items,
  about {HOURS} hours, in as many sittings as you like. Pages are public company and government
  pages. Some show public names, such as forum usernames or press contacts.

Your choice
  Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and your ratings
  will then not be used. You can ask to withdraw your ratings until the paper is submitted.

Risks
  There is no known risk beyond ordinary computer work. The pages are saved copies of public
  web pages, and the app does not connect to any website.

Your data
  The app records your ratings, any notes you write and the time spent, under the rater name
  you choose when you start it. Before anything is published, every rater name is replaced with
  a neutral code such as rater-07, so your chosen name never appears in the published data.
  Your ratings, under that code, may be published with the paper. The paper describes raters
  only as a group. Your real name is used only if you ask to be acknowledged. Your email stays
  private with the person who sent you this folder.

Payment
  As agreed with the person who sent you this folder.

Before you start, please reply by email to the person who sent you this folder with:
  1. "I have read this note and agree to take part, and to my ratings being used and published
     as described."
  2. Your general background, in a few words.
  3. Your experience with search or AI systems: none, some or a lot.
  4. Whether you have worked with them on any web-text extraction tool.
  5. Whether you want to be named in the acknowledgements or stay anonymous.
  6. The rater name you will use.

When you finish, email the results file the app names, and confirm that you rated alone and
did not use AI tools to decide.

How to run the app is in START_HERE.txt. What to judge is in HANDOUT.md.
