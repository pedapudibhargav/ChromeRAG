# Human rating set: post hoc robustness and disclosure analyses

**Post hoc.** Everything on this page was computed after all ratings were in and after the planned analysis had been run. None of it is in the analysis plan (PROTOCOL.md). It changes no rating, page, setting or tool. The planned result stays the primary result.

Unit: the plurality verdict of the raters per page (75 pages; equal top counts give a tie). Net = (pages where the first tool wins minus pages where it loses) / pages. Intervals: 95% percentile, site bootstrap (registrable domain of the corpus `site` field, 48 sites; 10,000 draws; seed 20261120), as in the planned analysis. Generated by `poc/human_set_posthoc.py`.

## History of the human set

Factual record from PROTOCOL.md, its git history and the author's status notes. Times are US Pacific. AI judge totals are primary (non-swapped) verdicts, ChromeRAG-baseline-tie; those marked post hoc were computed for this report.

**First draw (2026-11-01).** The set fixed in the protocol section "Human rating set (2026-11-01)" (commit `ecc643d`, 22:19) was drawn by `evaluators/author/build_bundle.py` with seed 20261101 from the 296 new pages: 75 main pages (article 31, service 23, forum 10, listing 6, landing 2, product 2, collection 1), 5 repeats and 5 practice pages. In drawing, the builder skipped candidate pages whose tool output was short (35 skipped), a selection on tool output that the later set dropped. Before the draw the GPT judge had already judged all 296 pages (results commit `01c16f3`, 21:27), and those totals had been seen: 158-114-24 against Trafilatura, 182-97-17 against Readability. Its verdicts on the drawn pages therefore existed; no record shows that totals for the drawn subset were computed at the time. Post hoc, on the 75 first-draw pages: 34-32-9 against Trafilatura, 47-25-3 against Readability.

**Why it was replaced (2026-11-02).** The first page collection had reached a site flagged as unsafe, and the set still held pages that could load live images. The amendment "offline pages and safety filter" (commit `4e5ea40`, 11:48) stripped links, images and remote styles from saved pages and added a mechanical safety filter that removed 8 of the 296 pages (2 of them in the first draw); it records a redraw from the remaining 288. The same day the amendment "new human rating set from approved sites" (commit `0683b94`, 17:22) replaced the set altogether. No rater received the first draw or the redraw; the first draw's package is kept privately, unsent. The 296-page results are reported unchanged elsewhere.

**Approved sites and swaps (2026-11-02).** `human_set/sites.json` lists 85 sites in 14 named groups, approved by the author by name before any contact; `approved_addresses.json` holds the 507 addresses that passed two text-only reviews (15 removed by the second). Collection gave 145 pages from 51 sites, 99 after the limit of 2 per site (`c4aced6`, 17:46). Before any rating, pages were removed with recorded reasons: 6 after review for work safety or usability (`00d2c8c`, 17:53); forums added at a share fixed in advance at 10 of 75, knowing forums suit ChromeRAG (`78cc507`, 17:59; `5405840`, 18:15); 2 automated forum posts (`4d685af`, 18:19); 2 terms pages (`70b7d93`, 18:39). The bundle seed was replaced after these removals; each seed-file hash is in PROTOCOL.md. The AI judge plan was fixed at `4f5ce3a` (18:52), and the three AI judges then judged the drawn set, whose totals were seen. Two tool-blind rules (all three tools under 80 words; page text over 8,000 words) were then adopted (`850cf98`, 19:14) and 5 drawn pages were swapped one for one (0069 to 0060, 0094 to 0101, 0130 to 0119, 0131 to 0007, 0137 to 0071; `5ebf4fb`). The 5 new pages were judged and all AI verdicts frozen (`3b4f43d`, 19:21); the verdicts on the 5 swapped-out pages remain in the `*.progress.jsonl` files. Rating began on 2026-11-04, after the remaining app and analysis amendments (2026-11-02 and 2026-11-03).
